# Validation Brief

> Module 2 · Validation. Frame the riskiest assumption, then build to test it. **This locks your scenario, no switching after M2.**

## Scenario

Scenario 03 · The Marketplace Trust Problem

## Riskiest assumption

_The one belief that, if false, sinks the idea._

Buyers won't book a brand-new, zero-review provider whatever the price because they have no on platform presense and proof built up.

## Hypothesis

> We believe **a trust panel, verification badges plus early social proof, on new-provider profiles, Links to Social media where these service providers can display their work or portfolio and connection with community, any off platform work and experience that they might want to highlight, give them a platform to tell the story of what they are doing , why and what their passion is** will cause **more first bookings for zero-review providers, more trust in knowing that the marketplace is doing a lot of vetting for the buyer on the sellers,  trasnforming service providers from numbers to humans who need a chance** for **buyers browsing new providers**. We'll know we're right when **the first-booking rate for new providers rises MoM , other metrics improbve as well like satisfaction score , ratings etc over time**.

## Risk type

- [x] Value (do they want it?)
- [x] Usability (can they use it?)
- [x] Feasibility (can we build it?)
- [x] Viability (should we?)

## Kill switch

_The result that would make you stop or pivot._

If verified badges and modifications to how the platform tells the stories about these users and their journey don't lift first bookings, trust isn't the barrier, pivot.

## The three ingredients

- **Real data (domain metrics):**
  - 0, Reviews on 41% of active provider profiles.
  - 2.3%, Booking rate for zero-review providers vs. 14% for reviewed.
  - 68%, Search → profile → exit without booking.
  - 19 days, Median time for a new provider's first booking.
  - −4%, QoQ booking growth: supply is growing, demand isn't.
- **User voice (verbatim quotes):**
  - "If there are no reviews, I assume something's wrong with them. I'll pay more for someone with a track record.", Buyer, churned at checkout
  - "I'm great at my job but I'll never get a review if no one books me first. It's a chicken-and-egg trap.", New provider, 0 bookings
  - "I wish I could see *something*, a verified ID, a portfolio, anything, before I commit money.", Buyer, abandoned search
  - "The established providers are booked out for weeks. I'd try someone new if I felt safe doing it.", Repeat buyer
- **Hypothesis (above):** ✓
